Privacy Policy

Soulmate Sketcher

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Soulmate Sketcher creates AI-generated soulmate portraits and accompanying readings based on information provided by users. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use the app.

1. Information You Provide

When you request a portrait, the app may send the following quiz answers to our backend service:

  • Name
  • Date of birth
  • Time of birth, if provided
  • Birth city, if provided
  • Preferred gender of the imagined soulmate
  • Preferred ethnicity of the imagined soulmate

Birth time and birth city are optional if you choose not to provide them. Please consider carefully what information you choose to submit.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your quiz answers to generate an AI-written reading and a corresponding illustrated portrait. The information is processed only as needed to provide the requested generation service.

Our backend sends the quiz answers to OpenAI's text generation service to create the reading and a short physical description. It then sends that generated physical description, together with a fixed artistic style prompt, to OpenAI's image generation service.

Your name and birth details are not included in the request sent to the image generation model, according to our current implementation.

3. Third-Party Services

OpenAI

OpenAI processes information on our behalf to generate readings and portraits. Your quiz answers and the resulting reading are sent to its text generation service. The generated physical description is sent to its image generation service.

Our backend does not save your quiz answers, reading text, or generated image to its own database or files. However, OpenAI's handling and retention of submitted information are governed by its applicable policies and service terms.

OpenAI states that API inputs and outputs are not used to train its models by default unless the customer opts in. Its standard abuse-monitoring logs may retain customer content for up to 30 days, subject to applicable exceptions and configured data-retention controls.

OpenAI privacy information: OpenAI Privacy Policy

OpenAI API data controls: OpenAI API Data Controls

Apple MapKit

When you search for a birth city in the app, the city search query is sent to Apple's MapKit service. This city-search request is separate from the portrait generation request to our backend.

Apple's handling of information is governed by its applicable privacy policies and service terms.

Apple Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage and Retention

Our backend processes quiz answers and generated results in memory while fulfilling your request. We do not intentionally save quiz answers, reading text, or generated images to our own database or files.

Our server logs record that a generation request occurred and may record API errors. These logs are not designed to contain your quiz answers or generated images.

The reading and portrait are held in memory on your device during the session. If you choose to save the portrait, the image is saved locally to your Photos library. Saving is optional.

OpenAI and Apple may process or retain information under their respective policies. We cannot promise deletion from third-party systems outside our control.

5. Photos and Device Permissions

If you choose to save a generated portrait, the app may request the necessary Photos permission. The image is saved to your device's Photos library. Saving a portrait does not upload it back to our backend.

You can manage Photos access through your device settings. You can also delete saved portraits from your Photos library yourself.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

If the app offers paid features or subscriptions, purchases are processed through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple handles payment information under its own policies. We do not receive your full payment card details through the app's portrait generation backend.

You can manage or cancel eligible subscriptions through your Apple Account subscription settings.

7. Data Security

We use a backend service to process generation requests and return results. Information is transmitted to the services needed to provide the app's features. No method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

8. Age Rating and Children's Privacy

Soulmate Sketcher's age rating varies by country or region and operating system version. The age rating displayed on the App Store in your location applies to your use of the app.

Soulmate Sketcher is intended for users who meet the applicable age rating requirements. Parents and guardians should review the app's age rating before allowing a child to use it.

The app processes information provided in the soulmate quiz, including a name and birth details when supplied, through our backend and third-party AI services to generate readings and portraits. Please do not submit personal information about a child unless you have the legal authority to do so.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us using the contact information in this Privacy Policy. We will review the request and take appropriate steps in accordance with applicable law.

9. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You may choose not to provide optional quiz information. You can stop using the app at any time and manage device permissions through your device settings.

Because our backend does not intentionally retain quiz answers, readings, or generated images, we generally do not have those items available for later retrieval or deletion from our own database. Third-party retention is subject to the relevant provider's policies.

For questions about privacy or requests concerning information processed through Soulmate Sketcher, contact us using the email address below.
